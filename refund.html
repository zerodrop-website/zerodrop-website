Refund & Support Policy

14-Day Money Back Guarantee

1. 14-Day Guarantee

We stand behind the engineering of ZeroDrop. If the software fails to reduce latency or resolve frametime stutter on your system, and our technical support team cannot resolve the issue, you are eligible for a full refund within 14 days of purchase.

2. How to Request a Refund

Refunds and technical support are handled directly via our verified Discord community. Simply open a private ticket in the #create-ticket channel on our Official Discord Server with:

  • Your purchase email and order / license key ID.
  • A brief description of the technical issue encountered.

Refunds are processed within 24 to 48 hours directly back to your original payment method (Card, PayPal, Apple Pay).

3. Technical Support Discord

All customers receive dedicated access to our technical support community where our team provides one-on-one assistance for custom game profiles, OBS streaming latency tuning, and display overclocking.
