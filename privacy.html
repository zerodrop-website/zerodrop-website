Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 — GDPR & CCPA Compliant

1. Data We Collect

We respect your privacy and adhere strictly to data minimization principles:

  • Hardware ID (HWID): An irreversible cryptographic SHA-256 hash derived from motherboard and CPU serials to validate software licenses. We never receive your raw serial numbers.
  • Email Address: Used solely by payment processors (Lemon Squeezy / Stripe) to deliver license keys and critical update alerts.
  • Telemetry: ZeroDrop does NOT track your browsing history, game activity, keystrokes, or personal files. In fact, our software explicitly disables Windows telemetry.

2. How We Store and Protect Your Data

Local license keys stored on your device are encrypted using Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI). Remote authentication occurs exclusively over encrypted TLS 1.3 connections.

3. Third-Party Payment Processors

Payment information is processed securely by Lemon Squeezy or Stripe as our Merchant of Record. We never see, store, or have access to your credit card numbers or banking credentials.

4. Your Rights (GDPR)

Under European Union GDPR regulations, you have the right to request deletion of your license record or access to associated records at any time by contacting our support desk.
